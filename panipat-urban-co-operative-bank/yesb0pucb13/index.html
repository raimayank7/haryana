How the code YESB0PUCB13 is built

YESB is the bank part: these are the letters of Yes Bank. Panipat Urban Co-operative Bank sends and receives payments through that bank, so its codes carry those letters. The fifth character is always the digit 0. PUCB13 is the part that names this branch.

The MICR code 126800002 is the number printed along the bottom of this branch's cheques. 126 is the city (the same three digits that begin the PIN code 126102), 800 is the bank and 002 is the branch.
